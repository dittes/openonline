About AVIF files

AVIF is a modern image format based on the AV1 video codec. It compresses better than JPEG and WebP and supports HDR and transparency.

Current versions of Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari decode AVIF natively.

What you can do here

  • Zoom with the mouse wheel or buttons, drag to pan
  • Switch between fit to screen and actual pixels
  • Transparent areas shown on a checkerboard
  • Rotate or flip, and save the result as a new file
  • Read camera data (EXIF): camera, lens, exposure, date and GPS position

Limitations

  • Saving a rotated or flipped copy re-encodes the image and drops its EXIF data.

Common questions

The image doesn’t display. Why?

Your browser may be too old to decode AVIF. Update it, or convert the image to JPG or PNG with aintob.com.

Is HDR shown correctly?

That depends on your browser and display. On non-HDR screens the image is tone-mapped.
